Privacy

Your images are processed entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. No files are uploaded to any server, and no images are stored by this website.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Image compression happens completely inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Will image quality be reduced?

The tool reduces file size while attempting to preserve as much visual quality as possible.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP and BMP images can be uploaded. Compressed images are downloaded in JPG/JPEG format.

Can I choose a custom target size?

Yes. Select "Custom" and enter any target size in KB.
